Status and Responsibilities

You will initially serve as Principal AI Reliability & Quality Engineer and you will
initially report to Steve Ratay as your supervisor. This is a FULL-TIME, EXEMPT
position.
Your job responsibilities will include, but are not limited to, the following:
Platform Reliability & SRE Culture
● Lead design and maintenance of highly available, scalable infrastructure with
99.9%+ uptime targets.
● Define and maintain SLOs, SLIs, and error budgets in partnership with
engineering and product teams to balance reliability investment with feature
velocity.
● Design and maintain the on-call program, escalation policies, runbook library, and
blameless post-mortem process.
● Establish comprehensive monitoring, alerting, and observability frameworks, and
drive proactive capacity planning to scale with business growth.
● Serve as the organizational authority on SRE principles, building a culture of
reliability through mentorship, training, production readiness reviews (PRRs),
chaos engineering exercises, and game days.

AI System Reliability & Evaluation
● Define reliability standards for AI-powered workflows and agentic systems,
including hallucination risk mitigation, response consistency, and structured
output guarantees.
● Establish AI-specific SLIs and SLOs, such as response accuracy, retrieval
relevance, structured output validity, tool invocation success rate, and workflow
completion reliability.
● Design and maintain automated AI evaluation pipelines using curated datasets,
regression prompts, and scenario simulations.
● Implement monitoring systems that detect AI drift, hallucination patterns, prompt
regressions, and model degradation in production.
● Develop adversarial testing and red-team frameworks to evaluate AI robustness
and safety.
● Partner with AI engineering teams to ensure agentic workflows meet
enterprise-grade reliability and explainability standards before production release.
Infrastructure, Automation & AI-Enhanced Operations

deployment patterns, configuration management, and environment provisioning.
● Own release engineering practices including canary deployments, blue/green
releases, and progressive rollouts.
● Lead automation initiatives to systematically eliminate operational toil.
● Apply AI coding assistants and prompt-driven development tools to accelerate
infrastructure engineering and improve code quality.
● Integrate AI-powered monitoring for predictive alerting, automated root cause
analysis, and intelligent incident triage.

Quality Engineering & Test Strategy

suites, and testability-first design into the SDLC.
● Own performance and load testing strategy across platform and AI workloads.
